LIVE CASE · THAILAND

Project: GreenBite enters Thailand

Bạn cần đề xuất cách tìm khách hàng B2B tại Thái Lan cho một doanh nghiệp Việt Nam trong ngành thực phẩm.

2 / 4 tasks
Brief từ Sales Manager: “We need 10 qualified B2B leads in Thailand. Prioritize distributors, healthy-food retailers and importers. Use public information and explain why each lead fits.”

Your deliverable

  1. Select target segment.
  2. Build a shortlist of 10 potential B2B partners.
  3. Record company, segment, evidence and fit rationale.
  4. Prepare 3 priority leads for outreach.
